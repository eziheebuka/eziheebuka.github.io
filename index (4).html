Profile

Data to decisions, from the field to the national dashboard

Entomologist and public health professional holding a PhD in Entomology and Forensic Studies, with over twelve years of field research and programme experience spanning Anopheles vector ecology, insecticide resistance, malaria transmission dynamics, disease surveillance, and monitoring and evaluation.

I combine hands on entomological field methods and vector control expertise with M&E systems design, strategic information management, and data analysis. My work translates field and surveillance data into recommendations that government bodies, UN agencies, and international donors can act on, including NMEP, NCDC, AFENET, PMI, Malaria Consortium, Global Fund, USAID supported programmes, MSF, and NAMRU-3.

Featured dashboard

Nigeria Malaria Control — Progress, Gaps & Priorities, 2009–2025

A national strategic information dashboard tracking sixteen years of insecticide treated net campaigns and the full malaria intervention mix across all 37 states and the FCT, drawing on DHS 2018, MIS 2021 and MIS 2025. It benchmarks the Kebbi model of full coverage, ranks state burden and trends, and sets out donor investment priorities for NMEP.
